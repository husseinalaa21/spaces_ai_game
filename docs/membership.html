Premium

$9.99 per month. Every cosmetic in Spaces, unlocked at once.

What you get

UniversesAll 9, including Cosmic, Aurora and Volcano.
Dot StylesAll 38, including Galaxy, Rainbow, Nebula and Starlight — with their hats, clothing and eyewear.
InstantlyNo Points needed, and nothing to unlock one at a time.
Everything newCosmetics added in future updates are included while the subscription is active.

Cosmetic only — deliberately

Premium changes nothing about how Spaces plays. It does not make your dot faster, larger, harder to eat or better at scoring, and it has no effect on anyone else. It is there for people who want their dot to look like something particular.

Subscription terms

  • $9.99 per month, charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Renews automatically at the same price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.
  • Manage or cancel in device Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the game under Settings → Manage subscription.
  • Cancelling stops future charges; Premium stays active until the end of the period already paid for, then premium cosmetics re-lock.

Restoring

On a new device, sign in with the same Apple Account and use Settings → Restore purchases. Premium is tied to your Apple Account, not to a device or to a Spacechat account.

Buying without an account

Purchases require signing in — with Apple or with a Spacechat phrase. Without an account there is nothing to attach a purchase to when you reinstall or change phones, and nothing to restore against. Playing requires no account.
