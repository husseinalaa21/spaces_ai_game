Common questions

I lost my recovery phrase

There is nothing we can do. The phrase is the account; it is never sent to us and we hold no copy to reissue. If you are still signed in on a device, open Settings → Recovery phrase and save it somewhere safe now.

I paid for Premium but it is not showing

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Premium is tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you are signed in to the same one you bought with.

How do I cancel Premium?

Device Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Settings → Manage subscription in the game. Cancelling stops future charges; Premium stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled entirely by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My Points disappeared after reinstalling

Points are a consumable purchase and Apple does not restore consumables. If your account has Spacechat cloud save your balance returns with your profile; otherwise it lives on the device.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything held on the device. For a Spacechat account and the data held against it, email us and we will delete it.
