Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

What Spaces stores, why it stores it, and what never leaves your device. This policy covers the Spaces — Dots Game iOS app, published by Spacechat LLC.

The short version

  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no data brokers.
  • We do not sell personal data, and never have.
  • You can play the entire game without an account.
  • Your progress stays on your device unless you sign in with a Spacechat account that has server storage.
  • We never see your payment details — Apple handles every purchase.

We do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, device advertising identifier, or browsing activity. The camera is used only when you choose to scan a Spacechat profile QR code; frames are processed on your device and are never uploaded or stored.

2. Accounts, and playing without one

Spaces is fully playable with no account. Signing in is required only to make a purchase — so it can be restored later — and to use Messages and Spacechat AI, which need a Spacechat account to address.

A Spacechat account is a recovery phrase rather than an email address or phone number. That phrase is the account: anyone holding it can sign in as you, and nobody — including us — can reissue it if it is lost. It is held in your device’s Keychain, marked device-only so it cannot travel in a backup, and removed entirely when you sign out.

3. Cloud save

Progress stays on your device by default. If you sign in with a Spacechat account that has server storage, your game profile is written into your own Spacechat account database when the game closes, so it follows you between devices. Accounts without server storage keep everything locally, and the game says so in Settings rather than failing quietly.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising. Data reaches only:

  • Spacechat (Spacechat LLC) — authentication, Messages, Spacechat AI, cloud save.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and all purchases and subscriptions, governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.

We may disclose information where legally required, or where necessary to protect the safety of a person or the integrity of the service.

5. Retention and deletion

Everything held on your device is removed when you delete the app. Signing out removes your recovery phrase and session from the device. To delete a Spacechat account and the data held against it, contact us below.

6. Children

Spaces is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Traffic to Spacechat is over HTTPS. Credentials are held in the iOS Keychain rather than plain storage, marked so they do not leave the device in a backup. No system is risk-free, but we do not collect data we do not need, which is the most reliable protection available.

8. Your rights

You can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, and withdraw consent by signing out or deleting the app. Depending where you live you may have further rights under the GDPR or CCPA; we apply these to everyone rather than by region.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note the change in the app. Continuing to use Spaces after a change means accepting the updated policy.
