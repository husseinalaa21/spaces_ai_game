Spaces — Dots Game

A dot in a white universe. Eat, grow, and become something else — then design a dot that is entirely your own.

One account, two apps

Spaces is built by Spacechat LLC and shares its accounts. Sign in with a Spacechat recovery phrase and the Messages and Spacechat AI pages in the game are the same conversations you see in the Spacechat app. You can also sign in with Apple, or play with no account at all — the game is complete either way.

What’s in it

PlayAbsorb collectibles and rival dots, complete forms, and level up through a white, gridded universe.
CustomizeDesign your own dot in the Dot Studio — paint it freehand, add icons, set its colour. Or pick from 38 Dot Styles and 9 Universes.
MessagesPrivate one-to-one chats and rooms, on your Spacechat account.
Spacechat AIThe same assistant built into Spacechat, inside the game.

Pricing in short

Free to download and free to play. Premium is an optional monthly subscription that unlocks every cosmetic at once, and Point Packs are optional one-off purchases. Nothing you can buy affects gameplay — see Pricing and Premium.

Privacy in short

No ads. No analytics SDKs. No trackers. No data sold, ever. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
