Points

Points are the in-game currency. They buy individual Universes and Dot Styles — 250 to 1,500 Points each — without a subscription.

You never have to buy Points. You earn them by playing: 10 to 20 for eating a collectible depending on rarity, 20 for completing a form, 15 for levelling up, and 10 to 30 from the daily reward. Earning is capped at 70 Points a day so the game stays a game rather than a grind. Buying is not capped.

What buying does not do

Nothing purchasable changes how the game plays. Premium and Points unlock appearance — colours, patterns, hats, eyewear, universe backdrops. There is no paid speed, size, scoring or matchmaking advantage, and there is no loot box or randomised purchase anywhere in Spaces.

Payment, renewal and refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see or store card details. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; cancel any time in device Settings, or from Settings → Manage subscription in the game. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Points are a consumable purchase. Consumables are not restorable by Apple’s design, so Points stay with the account that bought them.
