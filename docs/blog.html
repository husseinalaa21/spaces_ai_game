Notes on what is changing in Spaces.

16 September 2026

Design your own dot

The Dot Studio has landed. Paint your dot freehand, stick icons on it, set its base colour, and it is yours everywhere — the menu, the picker, and in the universe while you play. Artwork is stored relative to the dot’s own radius, which is why one design looks right on a 46-point swatch and on a full-size dot mid-round.

16 September 2026

Messages and Spacechat AI, inside the game

Sign in with your Spacechat recovery phrase and the Messages and Spacechat AI pages are the same account you already use in the Spacechat app — the same conversations, the same rooms. One phrase covers both. Prefer not to? Sign in with Apple and guest play both still work, and the game is complete without either.

16 September 2026

Point earning, rebalanced

Every reward now pays between 10 and 20 Points, with a 70-point daily ceiling on what playing can earn. The Value and Mega packs were repriced to be genuinely better value than the Starter pack — previously the middle tier paid slightly fewer Points per dollar than the cheapest one while carrying a “best value” label. It now pays double, and Mega pays triple.

16 September 2026

Flat dots

Every shadow behind a dot is gone — the ground shadow, the rim shading, the hat shadow, and the permanent glow that sat behind each dot and read like a smudge. Dots are flat, bright shapes now, and light blooms behind one only at the moment it eats something.
